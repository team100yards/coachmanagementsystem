COACH MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

🏆 Squad & Roster Hub 🎯 Interactive Tactics Studio 🎥 Broadcast Video Telestration 🤖 AI Match Report Studio 📋 Training Session Planner
THE CHALLENGE

The Problem: Fragmented Coaching Workflows

📁 Scattered Tools

Coaches juggle spreadsheets for rosters, screenshot apps for formations, and paper notes, causing data loss and administrative confusion.

⏱️ Administrative Burden

Manual player registration, medical forms, and attendance tracking consume hours each week that should be invested in player development.

🎬 Costly Video Software

Traditional telestration platforms require expensive licenses and complicated desktop setups, leaving academies without visual feedback.

📝 Inconsistent Post-Match Insights

Post-match observations are rarely structured into actionable tactical debriefs or shared effectively with the squad.

THE ECOSYSTEM

The Solution: One Unified Operating System

🏆 Squad & Multi-Team Hub

Centralized roster, staff directory, player photos, birthday alerts, and CSV import/export.

🎯 Tactics & Lineup Studio

Interactive pitch supporting 3v3 to 11v11, formation presets, and Game Changers bench.

🎥 Video Telestration

In-browser video scrubbing with spotlights, arrows, player tags, and tactical canvas.

🤖 AI Match Report Studio

Voice dictation notes converted into instant tactical summaries, player ratings & timelines.

📋 Session Planner & Drills

Drill catalog, training presets, attendance tracking, and micro-cycle periodization.

💬 Live Chat & Onboarding

Self-serve player registration links, WhatsApp sharing, and pinned coach announcements.

TACTICS & LINEUPS

Tactics Studio: Dynamic Formations & Lineups

Multi-Format Match Support

Engineered for 3-a-side, 5-a-side, 7-a-side, 9-a-side, and official 11-a-side match formats with automatic pitch scaling.

Formation Intelligence

Pre-configured tactical shapes (4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, 4-4-2 diamond) with standardized position codes for all 15 roles.

Game Changers (Bench) & Match Details

Enforces squad capacity rules, tracks out-of-squad players, and stores Opponent, Tournament, and Venue information.

VIDEO ANALYSIS

Broadcast-Grade Video Telestration

🎬 Millisecond Video Scrubbing

Upload MP4/WebM match footage with high-precision frame stepping, playback speed controls, and smooth scrubbing.

✏️ Telestration Toolkit

Powered by Fabric.js canvas: Spotlights, passing lane vectors, pressing zone boxes, freehand drawing, and player labels.

⏱️ Key Event Bookmarking

Timestamp key match moments: defensive triggers, counter-attacks, set pieces, and goals.

📸 Frame Snapshot Export

Capture annotated high-definition tactical frames instantly to distribute to players for visual debriefs.

POST-MATCH ANALYTICS

AI Match Report Studio

🎙️ Pitch-Side Voice Dictation

Coaches can record raw voice notes immediately after full-time with automated speech-to-text transcription.

🤖 Automated Tactical Breakdown

AI transforms match notes and scores into an executive debrief: Tactical strengths, defensive organization, and key growth points.

⭐ Match Timeline & Ratings

Chronological logging of goals, cards, and substitutions coupled with individual player performance ratings.

📄 Exportable Match Reports

Produce cleanly formatted match report cards ready for distribution to club management and players.

TRAINING & DRILLS

Session Planner: Structured Player Development

📚 Curated Drill Catalog

Pre-built drill database spanning Tactical, Technical, SSG (Small-Sided Games), Physical/SAQ, Set Pieces, and Recovery.

⚡ 1-Click Training Presets

Ready-made micro-cycles and practice templates to build high-intensity training days in seconds.

📊 Attendance & Load Metrics

Automated metrics for total training hours logged, session status, and squad attendance percentages.

🔍 Fast Search & Filter

Instant search by training topic, category, or drill objective to organize your seasonal curriculum.
